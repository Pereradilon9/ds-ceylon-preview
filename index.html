No-show without notice: 100% of the booking value. Requests must be in writing. Non-refundable hotel deposits, train and airline tickets may be charged separately.

How do your prices work?
  • Rates are in US dollars, per person, twin sharing, for a minimum of two adults.
  • Bed & breakfast is included; half board, full board and all-inclusive are optional supplements.
  • Entrance fees and activities are quoted separately and are indicative.
  • Rates are subject to hotel availability, taxes and supplier rates, and are confirmed when you book.
What should I wear to temples?
Clothing that covers shoulders and knees. Footwear is removed at sacred sites, so slip-on shoes help.
Are trains and safaris guaranteed?
Train tickets depend on availability. Park access, safari sightings, whale watching and excursions depend on availability, season and local conditions.
Can the route change?
Yes. Duration, overnight stays and routing can vary with flight times, travel dates, season and your interests. Travel times are estimates.
Can I change a confirmed booking?
Changes depend on availability and may incur extra charges. Refunds, where they apply, are made after cancellation charges and bank fees.
